Quick answers

Questions people keep asking

What does a fresher full stack developer earn in India?

Most fresher offers fall between three and six lakhs a year, and candidates with strong deployed projects often reach six to ten lakhs at product focused companies. It varies with company type, city and interview performance.

Does my stack decide my salary?

It influences the kind of employers you meet, but depth matters more. A strong developer in a quieter stack can out earn an average one in a famous stack.

How long does it take to become job ready?

Most dedicated learners need six to twelve months of steady practice. Ninety focused minutes a day with regular mentor feedback beats an occasional weekend marathon.

Are these numbers guaranteed?

No. They are indicative ranges based on how the Indian market has generally behaved. Always compare with live job postings and people in your own network.
